A DECISION WILL APPROACH JUSTICE IF AND ONLY IF

1. PROTECTION OF INDIVIDUAL RIGHTS (Particularly natural rights like life, liberty, property, privacy & integrity) WHILE CONSIDERING GROUP RIGHTS & ORGANIZATIONS’ (INSTITUTIONAL) RULES. 
2. IN CASE OF VIOLATION OF THE 1ST BALANCE BETWEEN ‘PROPORTIONALITY OR RETRIBUTION,’ REHABILITATION (OF VICTIM & EVEN CRIMINAL, BUT VICTIM SHOULD BE PRIORITIZED), & RESTORATION, IF POSSIBLE.
