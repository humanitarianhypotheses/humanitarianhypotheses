A DECISION WILL APPROACH JUSTICE IF AND ONLY IF

1. PROTECTION OF INDIVIDUAL RIGHTS (Particularly natural rights like life, liberty, property, privacy & integrity) WHILE CONSIDERING GROUP RIGHTS & ORGANIZATIONS’ (INSTITUTIONAL) RULES.
2. A PERSON LOOSES PARTS OF THEIR NATURAL RIGHTS LIKE LIBERTY & PRIVACY AFTER VIOLATING 1st PRINCIPAL EVEN THE CRIMINAL LOOSES LIFE RIGHT IN CASE OF TOO MUCH UNRESTORABLE HARM LIKE DEATH FOR PROFIT OR CORRUPTION BY HIGHER AUTHORITY OR VIOLATION OF PRIVATE CONTRACT. BUT PROPERTY RIGHTS WHETHER INTELLECTUAL OR DIGITAL OR PHYSICAL ARE ABSOLUTE BECUASE THERE IS A PERSMISSION (CONSENT) & HISTORY ASSOCIATED WITH PROPERTY. 
3. IN CASE OF VIOLATION OF THE 1ST () BY A HUMAN PUNISHMENT SHOULD BE GIVEN TO THAT PERSON WHICH SHOULD CONSIDER BALANCE BETWEEN "‘PROPORTIONALITY OR RETRIBUTION,’ REHABILITATION (OF VICTIM & EVEN CRIMINAL, BUT VICTIM SHOULD BE PRIORITIZED), & RESTORATION, IF POSSIBLE".
4. NO LOGICAL FALLACY OR BIAS, PREJUDICE OR MANIPULATION IN DECISION.
